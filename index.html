The System

Zero advisor effort.
Automatic revenue.

Two automated workflows handle everything from appointment detection to customer reply — without a single advisor touching a keyboard.

01
Appointment Detected
The moment a new appointment is created, AutoAdvisor wakes up and retrieves the customer's vehicle, service history, and shop interval rules.
02
Services Calculated
A proprietary gap calculator estimates current mileage, scores every applicable service by urgency, and selects the top 3 — ranked by how overdue they are, not just category.
03
Text Generated & Sent
Claude AI writes a personalized SMS using the customer's name, vehicle, and specific services due. Sent via Twilio within 2 minutes of booking — before the advisor arrives.
04
Reply Captured
Customers reply YES, specific numbers, or nothing. AutoAdvisor parses the reply, updates service decisions in real time, and sends a confirmation — all automatically.
05
Advisor Sees Results
The advisor dashboard shows which services were pre-approved via text before the customer walks in. No cold selling — just confirmed work ready to write up.
06
Analytics Tracked
Every interaction — approval, decline, no response — is logged with approval rates by service type. Managers see exactly what's working and what isn't.

SMS Messaging Policy

AutoAdvisor is a B2B software platform licensed to auto dealerships. AutoAdvisor does not send text messages directly to consumers — all SMS communications are sent by the dealership using AutoAdvisor's platform on their behalf.

How customer consent is obtained: Each dealership that licenses AutoAdvisor is responsible for obtaining proper consent from their customers prior to sending any automated text messages. Consent is typically collected at the point of appointment booking — either through the dealership's online scheduling system, at the service counter, or by phone — using language such as:

"By scheduling a service appointment, you consent to receive automated text message reminders and service recommendations from [Dealership Name]. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time."

AutoAdvisor provides dealerships with compliant consent language templates and requires all licensed shops to adhere to TCPA regulations and carrier guidelines as a condition of use. Customers may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message.
